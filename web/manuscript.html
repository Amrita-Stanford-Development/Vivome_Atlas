How to read a line

The source under each claim is the committed file its numbers come from; hover a number to see the exact field. The tags say what kind of evidence it is:

  • development used while the model was built
  • in-sample the model's one tuned weight was chosen on these data
  • post-hoc the labels had been read at an earlier scoring
  • internal held-out locked before development and scored once; never to be called external
  • simulated RNA RNA cells made to look like protein uploads, not protein
  • pending not measured yet

Earlier designs appear only as the problem a part of the served model fixes. The served model's answers without its second opinion are its own answer path, so a comparison with them is an ablation.

Abstract, claim by claim

  • Problem. Single-cell mass-spectrometry proteomics rarely comes with independent cell-type labels, while RNA atlases do; a search found no public blood protein set whose labels do not come from clustering or from RNA. research/benchmark/side-tests.md
  • Approach. A five-member encoder trained only on Tabula Sapiens blood RNA, applied to each upload after gene-wise scaling within it; per-class conformal sets give an answer at type, family or lineage level, or an abstention with its reason; a correlation to RNA type means is added to the best guess when the upload agrees with the encoder. service/model/v4_1/spec_v41.json, docs/service/projection-api.md
  • Results. Development, post-hoc and internal held-out figures above, each with its caveat; level with the best correctly scaled simple baselines out of sample, ahead of the integration tools. research/benchmark/baseline-corrections.md
  • Availability. An online service that takes DIA-NN, FragPipe and plain matrices, with numbered, frozen releases. web/atlas.html, web/js/atlas/service-client.js, service/model/MODEL_CARD.md

Introduction

The problem, as this project's data show it

  • Public blood single-cell proteomics labels are not independent of the data: Fulcher 2026's come from RNA label transfer plus cluster refinement, Khoury 2026's from clustering the same protein matrix, PBMC240's from weak clusters. research/benchmark/protocol-fulcher2026.md, research/benchmark/protocol-khoury2026.md, research/benchmark/side-tests.md
  • Each cell measures a small part of the gene space: a median of proteins per cell in PBMC240 and in Fulcher, against genes the model reads; of PBMC240's matrix is missing. research/notebook-outputs/astral_abstention/summary.json, service/model/evidence/workflow/pbmc240_trace_v41.json.gz
  • Canonical lineage markers are rarely measured: of markers audited, are outside the gene space, and none of those is detected in more than of cells in any of datasets. Gains must come from patterns over abundant proteins. research/notebook-outputs/marker_audit/detection.csv
  • Integration tools assume things these data break: a review of this project's first benchmark found several baselines handicapped by the setup (a shared-population assumption, a likelihood that cannot fit z-scores, degenerate CCA settings, rescaled inputs), corrected from each tool's own documentation. research/benchmark/baseline-corrections.md
  • A user needs a label, the level it can be trusted at, and a reason when there is none, from a service that needs no installation. docs/service/projection-api.md

Contributions

  1. An encoder trained on RNA only that transfers blood cell-type labels to mass-spectrometry single cells.
  2. An answer layer: per-class conformal sets, answers at type, family or lineage level, and four abstention reasons.
  3. A second opinion, correlation to RNA type means over each cell's measured genes, used only where an upload agrees with the encoder.
  4. A benchmark under one protocol with corrected baselines, pre-registered checks and a locked internal test.
  5. A versioned atlas and an online service with frozen releases.

Framing

  • The claim is label transfer with honest abstention, not a shared space where the two modalities mix: the encoder keeps RNA and protein apart (see Results) yet transfers labels. research/roadmap.md (revision of 2026-10-01, item 9)
  • "Zero-shot" applies to training only: protein development data helped choose the encoder's training recipe and the one fusion weight. research/notebook-outputs/v41_pretest/selection.json in-sample

Experimental procedures

The RNA reference

  • Tabula Sapiens blood: cells from donors, in types, labelled by the atlas's cell ontology classes. service/model/runtime/reference_metadata.csv, service/model/v4_1/spec_v41.json
  • The types sit in families and lineages, so an answer can be a type, a family or a lineage. service/model/v4_1/spec_v41.json (hierarchy)
  • The reference is uneven: neutrophils are of its cells (), and it holds myeloid dendritic cell and plasmacytoid dendritic cells. service/model/runtime/reference_metadata.csv
  • Training uses RNA only. Every donor contributed training cells; no donor was held out. research/notebook-run-history.md (entry 13)

The gene space

  • The union of proteomics gene lists ( genes), less immunoglobulin and T-cell receptor genes, gives the model's space of genes. service/model/evidence/feature_space_provenance.json

Reading an upload

  • Formats: DIA-NN reports, FragPipe TMT-Integrator tables and plain CSV or TSV matrices, up to cells and MB. service/pipeline/alignment.py, service/config.py
  • Gene names resolve through a frozen, offline HGNC table (symbols, Ensembl and UniProt accessions); a protein group naming several genes is dropped as ambiguous. service/pipeline/gene_ids.py
  • A matrix with no negative values and a median observed value above is read as linear and log2-transformed. service/pipeline/alignment.py, service/config.py
  • Rows naming the same gene merge by each cell's median of their observed values. service/pipeline/alignment.py
  • A cell with fewer than observed genes gets no answer (too little measured). The floor was set on simulations and is provisional. service/config.py simulated RNA
  • Each gene is z-scored within the upload over its observed values; unobserved genes are zero, with a mask the encoder reads. No RNA statistics are applied to the upload. service/pipeline/alignment.py
  • Smoothing is on: a cell's values become its own plus its nearest neighbours', on a graph of principal components of the z-scored upload; the mask is left as measured. service/pipeline/smoothing.py, service/model/v4_1/spec_v41.json

The encoder and its training

  • members with one architecture. The input is wide: the masked values and the mask over genes, and the mean and measured share of gene modules. Two blocks of and units lead to a -dimensional, unit-length latent; weights per member. service/pipeline/encoder.py, service/model/runtime/provenance.json
  • Training: epochs, batches of , learning rate , weight decay , early stopping after epochs without gain, dropout . research/notebook-outputs/nb2/nb1b_core.py, research/notebooks/v5_train.py
  • Each training cell keeps between and of its genes at random; validation uses . of batches keep the reference's composition; the rest hold a few types in random proportions, like narrow uploads, and are scaled as an upload is. research/notebook-outputs/nb2/nb1b_core.py
  • Losses: logit-adjusted cross-entropy on natural batches, a margin supervised contrastive loss (temperature , margin ), a hubness penalty, and agreement between two masked views of each cell. research/notebook-outputs/nb2/nb1b_core.py
  • Each member's probabilities are a softmax of its cosines to the type centroids over a fitted temperature ( to ); the ensemble averages the members' probabilities. service/model/v3_1/nb2_spec_v31.json, service/pipeline/ensemble.py

The answer layer

  • All types compete; restricting the label space is an opt-in request setting. service/pipeline/label_space.py, docs/service/projection-api.md
  • Per-class (Mondrian) conformal sets at α , a target coverage of : one threshold per type, from to (marginal ), fitted once on labelled RNA uploads. Nothing is fitted on the user's upload. service/model/v3_1/nb2_spec_v31.json, service/pipeline/calibration.py simulated RNA
  • Every non-empty set also holds the answer path's top type and the fused best guess's. service/pipeline/fusion.py
  • A cell whose members' mean best cosine to the reference cells is below (the 1st percentile of in-distribution simulated cells) is outside the supported region; the search over the reference is exact. service/pipeline/abstention.py, service/pipeline/search.py
  • One type in the set: a type answer. Types sharing a family: a family answer. Sharing a lineage: a lineage answer. Otherwise none. The four reasons for none: too little measured, outside the supported region, no type stands out, several types fit. service/pipeline/ensemble.py
  • The same upload gives the same answer: fixed thresholds, a fixed seed in smoothing and an exact search; no cells are drawn from the upload for calibration. service/pipeline/fusion.py, service/tests/test_pipeline_versions.py

The best guess and its second opinion

  • The reference is re-encoded with every type weighted equally, and the type means of the z-scored RNA are kept under the same statistics. service/pipeline/fusion.py, service/model/v4/
  • Second opinion: each cell's Pearson correlation to the type means over its own measured, unsmoothed genes. service/pipeline/fusion.py
  • Agreement: the share of an upload's cells whose top correlation names the encoder's top type. At or above , the guess is encoder (its smoothed and unsmoothed views, each) plus the rest from a softmax of the correlations at temperature . Below it, the encoder alone decides (weight against a flat term). service/model/v4_1/spec_v41.json
  • The encoder weight is the one setting chosen on protein data, on Fulcher 2026, under fixed rules. research/notebook-outputs/v41_pretest/rules.json, research/notebook-outputs/v41_pretest/locked_rule.json in-sample

The simulated RNA evaluation suite

  • uploads, cells, built from held-back RNA test cells with the missing-value patterns of real files, in six kinds: one type alone, a few types, many types, one lineage, PBMC-like and SCoPE2-like. research/notebook-outputs/v41_phase3/summary.json, research/notebook-outputs/v41_phase3/rules.json simulated RNA

The benchmark protocol

  • Every method gets the same RNA reference, its labels and the same query cells; protein labels are read only when scoring. research/benchmark/methodology.md
  • baselines: correlation to type means, PCA, Harmony, scANVI, MaxFuse, scGLUE and Seurat CCA transfer, with seeds 0 to 2 where a method is stochastic. research/benchmark/baselines_corrected/inputs.csv, benchmark/baselines_run.py
  • Baselines get the benchmark genes each query measures (SCoPE2 , PBMC240 , Fulcher ), each z-scored over its observed values, the RNA side with every type weighted equally. The model reads its own gene space: Fulcher genes. research/benchmark/baselines_corrected/inputs.csv, research/notebook-outputs/astral_abstention/summary.json
  • Rules: a shared k-nearest-neighbour transfer (k = 30, cosine, distance weighted), nearest centroid, and each tool's own transfer. research/benchmark/methodology.md
  • Corrections to the baselines were taken from each tool's documentation and committed before any corrected run was scored; nothing was tuned. research/benchmark/baseline-corrections.md
  • Statistics: stratified and paired bootstraps, 2,000 resamples, seed 0, 95% percentile intervals (every CI on this page). Held-out sets: one-sided permutation AUROC or a donor-blocked bootstrap. research/benchmark/harness.md, research/benchmark/protocol-heldout.md
  • Measures: balanced accuracy over the dataset's types; lymphoid and myeloid recall, reported together; committed share; correct when committed, judged at the level stated; conformal coverage. research/benchmark/methodology.md
  • "Ahead of the baselines" needs a lead of at least 3 points over every corrected arm, each paired CI above zero, on a sealed test. research/benchmark/protocol-sorted-cd4cd8.md

Pre-registration and data handling

  • Each protocol is frozen at the commit that adds it; changes are dated amendments. research/benchmark/protocol-heldout.md, research/benchmark/protocol-khoury2026.md
  • Loaders refuse to return a locked set's labels; each run writes its rules, with its script's sha256, before it starts. research/notebook-outputs/v41_pretest/rules.json, research/notebook-outputs/v41_phase3/rules.json
  • Verdicts are written before labels are read: the Khoury check of the served rule was to "carry over" or not, fixed in advance. research/benchmark/khoury2026_posthoc_v41_check/summary.json
  • Held-out sets were scored once, against model files frozen by sha256; the scorer refuses a second run. research/benchmark/heldout/hao2021/summary.json (frozen_files)

Implementation

  • Members exported to TorchScript and checked equal to the trained models; the reference search is exact. service/pipeline/encoder.py, service/pipeline/search.py
  • Each cell's response: its answer and level, its conformal set, the best guess, its similarity to the reference, and the reason when there is no answer. docs/service/projection-api.md
  • Frozen full responses, a development gate and three test suites guard the served behaviour; model files are listed with their sha256. research/benchmark/v41_dev_gate.json, service/model/v4_1/MANIFEST.json
  • The service runs on Google Cloud Run ( vCPU, at most instance, a -second timeout); the site on Cloudflare Pages. deploy/huggingface/PUBLISH.md, docs/web/hosting.md

Cross-modal support

The atlas holds the reference's RNA cells and SCoPE2's protein cells; a type is cross-modal when it has cells of both.

Protein cells in the atlas cover only part of the label space. Types marked RNA only have none there, so their place in the atlas is not a cross-modal result; how the model does on them with real protein data is the recall by type above and the benchmark. web/data/metadata_RNA_lat128.csv, web/data/metadata_PROT_lat128.csv

Discussion

What the evidence supports

  • Lineage-level transfer from RNA to protein: lymphoid recall and myeloid on Khoury. research/benchmark/khoury2026_posthoc_v41_check/summary.json post-hoc
  • Type-level results level with the best correctly scaled simple baselines out of sample, and ahead of the integration tools. research/benchmark/khoury2026_posthoc_v41_check/paired_bootstrap.csv post-hoc
  • What no baseline offers: an answer at a stated level, right of the time on Fulcher, a reason when there is none, and a service that needs no installation. research/benchmark/v41_development/confident.csv development

What it does not support yet

  • A lead over the baselines: the criterion needs a sealed test with independent labels (). research/benchmark/protocol-sorted-cd4cd8.md pending
  • Conformal coverage on protein: below its target ( on Fulcher against ). research/notebook-outputs/protein_coverage/coverage.json
  • Other organs: the colon, lung and lymph node references miss their development gates, so none is served: narrow uploads for the colon and the lung, protein pools for the lymph node. research/organ-references.md

Lessons for benchmarking

  • Correctly scaled simple baselines are strong: once the setup handicaps were fixed, PCA with neighbours matched the model on Khoury. research/benchmark/baseline-corrections.md
  • Label bugs change conclusions: SCoPE2's macrophage and monocyte labels were swapped on every cell in this project's copy until corrected, which had turned restricted scores into 100 minus the truth. research/benchmark/bugs-and-fixes.md (entry 8)

Threats to validity

  • No protein label used here is independent of the protein data or of RNA transfer. research/benchmark/side-tests.md
  • Fulcher is in-sample for the weight; Khoury has been read several times, including to choose the served model over an alternative. research/benchmark/khoury2026_posthoc_v42_check/summary.json post-hoc
  • The simulated test cells are on their fourth use. service/model/v4_1/spec_v41.json (evaluation)
  • The model reads more genes than the baselines ( against on Fulcher); scANVI trains on the query cells, which favours it. research/benchmark/results.md, research/benchmark/known-limitations.md
  • The reference's labels are imperfect in the T, NK and myeloid blocks, and it holds few DCs; Khoury has two donors. research/notebook-outputs/v5_labels/audit.json, research/benchmark/protocol-khoury2026.md

Questions a reviewer will ask

  • Are the baselines fair? Their setup was reviewed and corrected from each tool's documentation before scoring, with setup checks recorded; but the model reads more genes than they do, and scANVI trains on the query cells. research/benchmark/baseline-corrections.md, research/benchmark/baselines_corrected/setup_checks.csv
  • Is Khoury really out of sample? No method was developed on it, but its labels have been read since its first scoring, so every Khoury figure here is post-hoc. research/benchmark/protocol-khoury2026.md
  • Are the labels independent? Not on Fulcher, Khoury or PBMC240; that is why the external test uses sorted cells. research/benchmark/side-tests.md
  • Is the simulated suite reused? Yes, its test cells are on their fourth use; it guards against regressions and is not used as evidence of a protein gain. service/model/v4_1/spec_v41.json
  • Is coverage guaranteed? No: on the simulated suite and on Fulcher, against a target of , so the paper calls it approximate. research/notebook-outputs/v41_phase3/summary.json, research/notebook-outputs/protein_coverage/coverage.json
  • Why not train on paired RNA and protein? The project has no RNA and mass-spectrometry measurements of the same blood cells; the roadmap names such bridge data as the next step. CITE-seq measures a panel of surface antibodies, not the proteome. research/roadmap.md (revision of 2026-10-04)
  • Does a shared latent space mix the modalities? No, and the paper does not claim it: the probe tells them apart almost always. service/model/evidence/v3_1_tables/modality_probe.json
