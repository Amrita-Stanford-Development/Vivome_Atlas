    How these frames are made

    Each frame is a still from the film itself, rendered by HyperFrames from its compositions in the repository's motion/ folder: HTML scenes animated with GSAP over the site's own living water and particles, drawn by three.js on the film's clock. Manim draws the answers scene, and FFmpeg grades each still.

    No number in a frame is typed in. When the frames are rendered, each one is read from the atlas's data files through the same rules as every page, and a record that is not measured yet reads Pending. Each still names its numbers and their sources, and the site's tests fail when a record no longer matches its still.
