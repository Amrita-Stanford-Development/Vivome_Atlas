The timeline

The project's milestones, oldest first, each with the topic it belongs to. A milestone of a written topic opens its step.

  1. 2025-08-17 The website

    The repository's first commit, 307e483: the atlas's data and its first pages.

  2. 2026-09-23 Testing the model

    The benchmark rebuilt under fixed rules, so every method is compared fairly.

  3. 2026-09-24 The upload service

    The service's encoder fixed: run end to end on SCoPE2, it now gives the model's own embedding.

  4. 2026-09-29 Testing the model

    The benchmark scored again over several seeds: the lead over scANVI belonged to the one seed that shipped, not to the model's design, and the records were corrected.

  5. 2026-09-30 The website

    The redesign began: the living background and the card with the blue halo became every page's look.

  6. 2026-09-30 Testing the model

    The Khoury set registered as a sealed final test, its labels locked away.

  7. 2026-10-01 The cell model and its versions

    v3.1 became the service's default: an ensemble of models that answers at several levels, with a best guess.

  8. 2026-10-02 The website

    The public site went live on Cloudflare Pages.

  9. 2026-10-03 Testing the model

    The Khoury set unsealed and scored once.

  10. 2026-10-03 How we work

    A review of the baselines, asked for by the owner, found our own setup had held them back. The corrections were committed before any corrected result was scored.

  11. 2026-10-04 The data

    SCoPE2's labels corrected: our copy had macrophage and monocyte swapped on every cell. Every table that used them was scored again.

  12. 2026-10-04 The cell model and its versions

    v4 passed its gate: v3.1's answers, with v4's best guess beside them, served on request.

  13. 2026-10-06 The cell model and its versions

    v4.1: the encoder's weight chosen on the Fulcher set under fixed rules, then checked once on the Khoury set.

  14. 2026-10-15 The data

    The data's roles set: most of it became development data, a fixed part was held out as the internal test, and external testing waits for sorted cells.

  15. 2026-10-15 The cell model and its versions

    v4.1 made the service's default.

  16. 2026-10-16 The cell model and its versions

    v4.2 served.

  17. 2026-10-16 The website

    The Workflow page: how VivOME works from start to end, following one real run.

  18. 2026-10-17 The cell model and its versions

    v4.1 served again, after v4.2 fell short on the Khoury set.

  19. 2026-10-18 Other organs

    Organ references began, the large intestine first. It closed NO GO the same day.

  20. 2026-10-20 Other organs

    The second versions of the lung and the lymph node: both NO GO, each by one gate.

  21. 2026-10-21 The upload service

    The service's revision 00006-rkt went live on Cloud Run, then the site, after reviews of their security and speed.

  22. 2026-10-26 The website

    The beta's sign-in: an invited email and one password in front of every page.

  23. 2026-10-27 The data

    The requests to two labs for their sorted cells, the planned external test, checked and sent.

  24. 2026-10-27 Testing the model

    PXD064083, sorted single T and B cells, scored once as a sealed test: it passed.

  25. 2026-10-28 Raw or log

    The raw-or-log plan approved, and Step 1 gathered the bank of real tables.

  26. 2026-10-28 How we work

    Diagnostics first: the raw-or-log work begins with a bank of real tables, and every step after it ends with runs on that bank, against expectations written before.

  27. 2026-11-03 Raw or log

    Phase A went live: an uploader can say how their file is written.

  28. 2026-11-03 Other organs

    Organ work paused until the raw-or-log work is finished.

  29. 2026-11-07 Raw or log

    B4 set the detector's thresholds.

  30. 2026-11-09 Raw or log

    B7 scored the sealed test once: the promise broke for small log tables, and the owner chose rework, then a fresh test.

Where the full records are

This section is written from the project's own records, and each step names the files it draws on. The records themselves are in the repository:

  • research/todo.md: the work list, and its changelog, one dated row for each piece of work.
  • research/notebook-run-history.md: the run history, an entry for each run, with what it found.
  • research/: the plans, the protocols of the tests, and each run's notes and report.
  • docs/: how each part of the site and the service works today.
