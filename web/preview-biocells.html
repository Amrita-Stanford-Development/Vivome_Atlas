VivOME

Two omes. One living space.

A versioned atlas that places RNA and protein measurements of single cells in one shared space, and says so when it can't.

The landing's first screen, with the objects in the water. Scroll to see each one up close.

The specimens.

Every object here is built in code. The cells and molecules follow their measured shapes; the threads are simple drawn shapes. Nothing is downloaded. Drag one to turn it.

In the water above, platelets are drawn 1.5 times larger so they stay visible, and the molecules are drawn hundreds to thousands of times larger than they would be beside the cells. The mix keeps the order of real blood, red cells most and white cells fewest, but not its ratio.

DNA double helix

2 nm across, 10.5 base pairs a turn

B-form and right-handed. The two backbones sit 144° apart, not 180°, which opens the wide major groove and the narrow minor groove. The pairs, A-T and G-C, are coloured by base.

Chromatin

Nucleosomes about 10 nm across

How DNA rolls up in a nucleus. It winds about 1.65 turns, left-handed, round each core of eight histone proteins, then runs on as linker DNA to the next: beads on a string. The double helix runs all the way round.

Coiled thread

A simple shape, not a measured one

A thread wound into a loose coil along a gently bending line, unwinding to a free end at each side.

Long thread

A simple shape, not a measured one

A long, thin thread drifting in gentle curves, thinning at its tips.

Long cell

Spindle-shaped

A long, thin cell, tapering to each end and gently bent, with a long nucleus. Fibroblasts and smooth muscle cells have this shape.

Red blood cell

7.8 µm across, 2.6 µm at the rim, 0.8 µm at the centre

The biconcave disc is the Evans and Fung equation for a resting red cell, spun into 3D: thick at the rim, thin at the centre.

Platelet

About 2.5 µm across, under 1 µm thick

A small, slightly lumpy disc. Its granules show through the membrane.

Lymphocyte

About 8 µm across

A large round nucleus of clumped chromatin fills most of the cell, inside a thin rim of cytoplasm. The surface is covered in short microvilli.

Monocyte

About 17 µm across, the largest white cell

A kidney-shaped nucleus, a ruffled membrane, and a wide cytoplasm with a few clear vacuoles.

Neutrophil

About 13 µm across

A nucleus of 3 to 5 lobes joined by thin strands, in a cytoplasm full of fine granules. This one has 4.
